LightSense switches your lights on when someone is in the room and it's dark, then off when they leave. Watch and control everything from a web dashboard.

Get started See how it works

From movement to light in three steps

1
Sense

A PIR sensor detects motion while a light sensor measures how bright the room is.

2
Decide

The ESP32 compares readings with your rules, such as "on below 300 lux when occupied".

3
Switch

A relay turns the lamp on or off, and the event appears in your dashboard.

Everything your lighting needs to run itself

Built from affordable, proven components

All parts are easy to find and low cost.

Controller
ESP32

Wi-Fi microcontroller that runs the logic.

Motion
HC-SR501 PIR

Detects movement in the room.

Light
BH1750 sensor

Measures ambient light in lux.

Energy
PZEM-004T

Reads voltage, current and power.

Time
DS3231 RTC

Keeps accurate time for schedules.

Switching
5V relay module

Safely switches the lamp circuit.

Display
OLED 0.96 in

Shows local status at a glance.

Software
Web dashboard

Live view and controls in the browser.
